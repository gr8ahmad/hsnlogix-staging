process

How We Work

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We learn about your store, your goals and what is holding growth back.

  2. 02

    Audit and scoping

    We review your current store, theme and apps, then define exactly what needs to be built or fixed.

  3. 03

    Proposal and timeline

    You get a clear scope, price and delivery timeline before any work starts.

  4. 04

    Build

    Development happens on a separate copy of your theme, so your live store keeps running as normal.

  5. 05

    Testing and review

    We test across devices and browsers and check speed and checkout before anything goes live. You review and approve.

  6. 06

    Launch and support

    We launch, monitor the first days after release and hand over what you need to manage the store. Ongoing support is available if you want it.
