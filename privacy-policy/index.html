Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

HSNLOGIX (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates hsnlogix.com. This page explains what data is collected when you visit this site, how it is used, and the controls you have over it.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use it. Analytics only activates if you choose to accept cookies using the banner shown on your first visit. If you decline, no analytics data is collected.

When you make a choice, it is stored in your browser (localStorage). You can change your choice at any time by clicking “Cookie settings” in the footer of any page.

Advertising features (ad targeting, remarketing) are not used. Analytics data collected through GA4 is subject to Google’s privacy policy.

Contact Information

If you book a call or reach out by email, you provide your name and email address voluntarily. We use this only to respond to your enquiry or schedule a consultation. We do not share this with third parties.

Third-Party Embeds

Cal.com is used for scheduling calls. When you open the booking widget, Cal.com may set its own cookies or store session data in your browser. Their data handling is governed by the Cal.com privacy policy.

Web3Forms is used to receive contact form submissions from this site. When you submit the “Discuss Your Store” form, your details are sent to Web3Forms’ servers for delivery. Their data handling is governed by the Web3Forms privacy policy.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not use advertising pixels, retargeting tags, or any tracking other than the analytics described above (and only with your consent).

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold. Contact us at hussain@hsnlogix.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions? Email us at hussain@hsnlogix.com.
