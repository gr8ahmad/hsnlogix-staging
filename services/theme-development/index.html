Custom Theme Development

A theme built around your brand and your business, not a template with your logo dropped in. Custom Shopify theme development from scratch, or a deep rebuild of what you already have.

Start your custom theme

Why off-the-shelf themes hold your store back

Off-the-shelf themes are built to work for thousands of different stores, which means they're rarely built well for any one of them. You end up stacking apps to get functionality the theme doesn't have, fighting the theme's structure to get the layout you actually want, and carrying page weight from features you'll never use. A custom theme is built around exactly what your store needs — nothing more, nothing forced into place.

What's Included

  • Custom theme design and development, built from scratch or rebuilt from an existing theme
  • Liquid-based custom sections and functionality (no unnecessary app dependencies)
  • Mobile-first, performance-conscious build from day one
  • Design system consistent with your brand across every page
  • Scalable structure that supports adding new features as your store grows

Why a custom Shopify theme pays off

A generic theme can only take a store so far. Off-the-shelf templates are built to work for thousands of different businesses at once, which means they rarely fit any one brand precisely, and every unused feature they carry still adds weight to the page. A custom theme is built around what your store actually needs, nothing more, which is often the difference between a store that merely looks fine and one that’s fast enough and clear enough to convert.

How It Works

1

Discovery: understand your brand, product catalog, and the specific functionality the theme needs to support.

2

Design direction and approval — sign off on the visual approach before a line of code is written.

3

Custom Liquid build: mobile-first, performance-conscious, built around your store's exact requirements.

4

QA across devices and browsers before anything touches your live store.

5

Launch with documentation so your team can make basic edits confidently without needing a developer for routine changes.
