Platform Migration

Move to Shopify without losing what you've already built. Seamless migration from WooCommerce, Wix, Magento, or any other platform — preserving your SEO rankings, customer data, and order history.

Plan your migration

Why platform migrations fail without a careful handoff plan

Migrating platforms is risky if it's not handled carefully: broken URLs tank your search rankings overnight, customer accounts and order history get lost, and a rushed migration means launching a store that's missing features your old one had. Most migration horror stories come from treating it as a simple content copy instead of a full technical transition.

What's Included

  • Full product, customer, and order history migration
  • 301 redirect mapping from old URLs to new Shopify URLs, protecting existing SEO rankings
  • Content and image migration (product descriptions, blog posts, media)
  • Custom theme build matching or improving on your current site's design and functionality
  • Pre-launch QA against the old site to confirm nothing was missed
  • Post-launch monitoring for redirect and indexing issues

Why every URL and record needs to move intact

A platform migration carries real risk if it’s handled carelessly: lost SEO rankings from broken URLs and missing redirects, lost customer data from an incomplete transfer, or downtime during a sales window the business can’t afford to lose. None of that is inherent to switching platforms — it’s what happens when a migration is rushed or done without a proper redirect and data-mapping plan. A properly managed migration moves everything that matters, product data, customers, order history, and search equity, without the store ever going dark.

How It Works

1

Audit your current store's full structure — products, URLs, content, and integrations.

2

Build the new Shopify store and migrate product, customer, and order data.

3

Map every old URL to its new Shopify equivalent, protecting existing search rankings.

4

Test extensively against the old site before DNS cutover.

5

Launch and monitor for the first weeks to catch any redirect or indexing issues.
