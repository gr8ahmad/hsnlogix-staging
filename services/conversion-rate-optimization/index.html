Why most stores can't tell where they're losing buyers

Most stores lose the majority of visitors at product pages, cart, or checkout, and never find out why. Guessing at fixes wastes time and can hurt conversion further.

What's Included

  • Funnel analysis: where visitors drop off and why
  • Product page optimization (layout, copy, social proof, urgency elements)
  • Cart and checkout friction review
  • A/B testing setup for key pages and elements
  • Mobile-specific UX fixes (most D2C traffic is mobile)
  • Trust-signal audit: reviews, guarantees, shipping/return clarity

Why fixing conversion is cheaper than buying more traffic

Getting more traffic to a store that isn’t converting is expensive. The better path is to understand why the traffic you already have isn’t buying — and in most cases, the answers are measurable. Drop-off points in the funnel, friction in the checkout flow, product pages that don’t build enough confidence to complete a purchase: these are patterns that show up in data and in UX, and they’re fixable. CRO isn’t about guessing which button color converts better. It’s about diagnosing the actual reasons a visitor doesn’t buy, then removing those barriers one by one.

If catalog structure is part of what’s creating friction — inconsistent variants, collection filters that don’t help customers narrow down, product data that doesn’t answer the right questions — Product & Collection Setup is the right starting point.

1
