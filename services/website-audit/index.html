Website Audit & Performance

Your store's speed is costing you sales. Get a full audit of what's slowing you down, and a prioritized fix list that actually moves the needle.

Get your store audited

The Problem

Slow load times, bloated apps, and unoptimized code quietly bleed conversions. Most stores are running 15–20 apps they've stopped using, images that were never compressed, and render-blocking scripts nobody's touched in years.

What's Included

  • Full Core Web Vitals audit (LCP, CLS, TBT) on both desktop and mobile
  • App audit: identify unused, redundant, or conflicting apps dragging down load time
  • Image and asset optimization review
  • Theme code review for render-blocking scripts, unused CSS/JS, and legacy bloat
  • Checkout and cart flow performance check
  • Prioritized action report ranked by effort-to-impact, not just a raw score dump

Why It Matters

Speed is a revenue lever, not a technical detail. Google’s own data shows that a one-second improvement in mobile load time can lift conversion rates by up to 27%. But most Shopify store owners never get a clear picture of where their performance budget is actually going — they get a raw PageSpeed score and a list of flags with no indication of which ones actually affect real users. This audit gives you a concrete picture of what’s slow, what’s redundant, and what to fix first.

Once you have a prioritized fix list, Theme Optimization & Responsiveness covers the implementation side — fixing the specific performance issues the audit surfaces.

How It Works

Audit (2–3 days) → findings report with screenshots and benchmarks → optional fix implementation, quoted separately once scope is clear.
