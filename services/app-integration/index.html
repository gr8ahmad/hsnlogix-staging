App Integration & Custom Features

Your theme wasn't built to do everything your business needs. Custom app integrations and bespoke features that extend Shopify past its default and app-store limits, without bloating your store.

Get a custom integration built

The Problem

Most solutions from the App Store come bundled with features you don't need, scripts that slow your site down, and monthly fees that add up. When your business needs something specific — a custom subscription flow, a unique product configurator, an integration with your fulfillment or CRM system — off-the-shelf apps either don't fit or force you to compromise on how the feature actually works.

What's Included

  • Custom app integrations (subscriptions, loyalty, fulfillment, CRM, ERP, and other third-party systems)
  • Bespoke on-theme features built in native Liquid instead of a bolted-on app
  • API integrations connecting Shopify to external tools and internal systems
  • Replacing bloated multi-app setups with a single custom-built feature
  • Performance-conscious implementation — no unnecessary scripts or render-blocking code

Why It Matters

Every app added to a store brings its own code, and stacking several of them is one of the most common reasons Shopify stores slow down over time. Custom-built integrations and features do exactly what a store needs without the extra weight of a general-purpose app, which keeps the store both more capable and faster than bolting on more off-the-shelf tools.

How It Works

Scope the exact functionality needed → decide whether it's better built as a lean custom integration or a targeted app connection → implement and test against your live store → handoff with documentation so your team knows how to manage it.
