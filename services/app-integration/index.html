App Integration & Custom Features

Custom development for stores that need functionality no theme or app provides out of the box, for businesses that have a specific workflow, integration, or feature in mind and need it built correctly rather than bolted on.

What's Included

  • Third-party app integrations
  • Custom Liquid code
  • Bespoke features built specifically for your store
  • Functionality that goes beyond what themes offer out of the box

Why It Matters

Every app added to a store brings its own code, and stacking several of them is one of the most common reasons Shopify stores slow down over time. Custom-built integrations and features do exactly what a store needs without the extra weight of a general-purpose app, which keeps the store both more capable and faster than bolting on more off-the-shelf tools.
